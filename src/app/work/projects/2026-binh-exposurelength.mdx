title: "Exposure-Length Trade-off Optimization for Obstacle-Aware Intrusion Paths in Heterogeneous Wireless Sensor Networks"
summary: "We propose OG-MOEA/D to jointly maximize sensing exposure and minimize path length in obstacle-constrained heterogeneous wireless sensor networks, using a fixed-grid path representation, obstacle-aware feasibility checking, and adaptive repair. Controlled 2D simulations show higher hypervolume and lower IGD than MOEA/D and NSGA-II across four network sizes. Accepted at The Journal of Supercomputing - Q2, IF: 3.5."
link: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01606"
